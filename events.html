Calendar not appearing?

Sign in as uhaiaa@hawaii.edu on a computer, open Google Calendar's Settings and sharing, then under Access permissions for events choose Make available to public and See all event details. If the option is unavailable, a UH Google Workspace administrator must enable public sharing.
